SeaCal — Privacy Policy

Effective date: 20 May 2026  ·  Last updated: 20 May 2026

Plain-English summary Your maritime records — sea time, certificates, days, and your profile — never leave your device. We collect only anonymous, non-identifiable usage analytics and serve standard mobile ads. Neither has any access to the data you enter into SeaCal.

1. Who We Are

SeaCal is developed and published by Gavin Kerr, Ireland. For the purposes of the EU General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR) and the Irish Data Protection Acts 1988–2018, Gavin Kerr is the data controller for any personal data processed in connection with this application.

For users in the United Kingdom, this policy also applies under the UK General Data Protection Regulation (UK GDPR) as retained and amended by the Data Protection Act 2018. The UK GDPR mirrors the EU GDPR in structure and rights; all obligations and protections described in this policy apply equally to UK residents.

Contact: seacal.app@gmail.com

2. Data That Never Leaves Your Device

All of the following is stored exclusively using your device's local storage. It is never transmitted to our servers, third parties, or any cloud service:

  • Sea time voyage records (ships, ranks, departments, dates)
  • Certificate details and any attached images or PDFs
  • In-country and out-of-country day entries
  • Personal profile (name, rank, department, COC history)
  • Countdown timers and packing lists
  • App settings, preferences, and theme choices

When you use the built-in backup feature, an export file is created on your device and shared only to destinations you explicitly select (e-mail, iCloud, Google Drive, etc.). We never receive or have access to a copy of your backup.

3. Data We Do Collect

3a. Anonymous Usage Analytics — Google Firebase Analytics

We use Google Firebase Analytics to understand how the app is used in aggregate so we can improve it. The data collected is anonymous and non-identifiable; it includes:

  • Which screens are visited and which features are used (the fact a screen was opened — not what you typed or entered)
  • Session duration and app open / close events
  • Device model, operating system version, and app version
  • Country or region (derived from IP address — never precise location)
  • A randomly generated Firebase installation identifier (reset when you reinstall the app; not linked to your name or identity)

Firebase Analytics does not receive any maritime data described in Section 2. Google processes this data under its own privacy policy (firebase.google.com/support/privacy).

3b. Advertising — Google AdMob

SeaCal is free to use and is supported by advertising served by Google AdMob. To serve relevant ads, Google may use:

  • iOS: your device's Advertising Identifier (IDFA), but only if you grant permission via the App Tracking Transparency (ATT) prompt shown on first launch. If you decline, only non-personalised ads are shown.
  • Android: your device's Google Advertising ID (GAID). You can reset or delete this in your device's Google settings.
  • Approximate location (country / region) and device type.

AdMob does not receive any of the maritime data you enter into SeaCal. Google's advertising privacy policy is at policies.google.com/privacy.

How to opt out of personalised ads:

  • iOS: Settings → Privacy & Security → Tracking → disable SeaCal; or Settings → Privacy & Security → Apple Advertising → turn off Personalised Ads.
  • Android: Settings → Google → Ads → Delete advertising ID.

3c. App Configuration Check

Once every seven days the app makes a brief outbound HTTPS request to fetch a small configuration file hosted on GitHub Pages. This request contains no personal data — it carries no cookies, user identifiers, or content from your records. It is used only to confirm the app is up to date and whether certain features (such as ads) are enabled.

5. Legal Basis for Processing (EU GDPR & UK GDPR — Article 6)

  • Legitimate interests (Art. 6(1)(f)) — anonymous usage analytics used to understand feature adoption and fix issues, where this does not override your fundamental rights and freedoms.
  • Consent (Art. 6(1)(a)) — personalised advertising on iOS, obtained via the App Tracking Transparency prompt before any advertising identifier is read. You may withdraw consent at any time through your device privacy settings without affecting any prior use of the app.

6. Data Retention

  • Your maritime data: retained on your device until you delete the app or clear its storage. We hold no copy.
  • Firebase Analytics data: retained by Google for up to 14 months by default, then automatically deleted.
  • AdMob data: retained by Google in accordance with its own data retention schedule.

7. International Data Transfers

Anonymous analytics and advertising data processed by Google may be transferred to and stored on servers outside the European Economic Area (EEA). Google complies with EU data transfer requirements through Standard Contractual Clauses (SCCs) approved by the European Commission.

For users in the United Kingdom, data transferred outside the UK is governed separately under UK GDPR. Google uses either UK International Data Transfer Agreements (IDTAs) or the UK Addendum to the EU Standard Contractual Clauses, both approved by the UK Information Commissioner's Office (ICO), to safeguard transfers of UK residents' data to servers outside the UK.

8. Your Rights Under GDPR

As a resident of the EU or UK you have the following rights. To exercise any of them, contact us at seacal.app@gmail.com. We will respond within 30 days.

  • Right of access — request a copy of any personal data we hold about you.
  • Right to erasure ("right to be forgotten") — request deletion of your data. For data on your device, delete the app. For Firebase / AdMob data, contact us and we will submit a deletion request to Google on your behalf.
  • Right to restriction — request that we limit how we process your data while a dispute is resolved.
  • Right to object — object to processing based on legitimate interests (i.e. analytics). We will cease unless we can demonstrate compelling grounds.
  • Right to data portability — receive your on-device data in a machine-readable format via the app's built-in export feature.
  • Right to withdraw consent — disable ad tracking in your device settings at any time.
  • Right to lodge a complaint — depending on where you are located, you may complain to your national supervisory authority:
    • EU / Ireland: Data Protection Commission (DPC) — dataprotection.ie
    • United Kingdom: Information Commissioner's Office (ICO) — ico.org.uk

9. Children's Privacy

SeaCal is designed for maritime professionals aged 16 and over. We do not knowingly collect personal data from anyone under 16. If you believe a child has provided personal data through the app, please contact us at seacal.app@gmail.com and we will take steps to delete it promptly.
