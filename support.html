Frequently Asked Questions

Is my data private?

Yes. All your sea time, certificates, and day records are stored only on your device — nothing is uploaded to us. See the full Privacy Policy for details.

Does SeaCal work offline?

Yes, all core features work fully offline. The app checks in with a small configuration file roughly once a week (for version/announcement info), but you can keep logging voyages, certificates, and days without a connection.

How do I add or scan a certificate?

Open the Certs tab, select a certificate, and use the camera or photo library option to attach a scan or photo. Camera and photo library access is used only to save that image locally to your device.

How do I back up or transfer my data to a new phone?

Go to Profile → Backup and export a backup file, which you can share to email, iCloud, Google Drive, or another app of your choice. Restore it from the same menu on your new device.

Why do I see ads?

SeaCal is free to use and supported by ads shown through Google AdMob. You may see a permission prompt for ad personalisation on first launch — declining it does not affect any app functionality.

How do I request my data be deleted?

Since your maritime records live only on your device, deleting the app removes them. For anonymous analytics/advertising data held by Google, email us and we'll submit a deletion request on your behalf — see the Privacy Policy for full details on your rights.

I found a bug or have a feature request

We'd love to hear it — email us with your device model, iOS/Android version, and what happened, and we'll look into it.
